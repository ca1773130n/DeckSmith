# v2 bespoke scenes: the build, and three decks through it

2026-10-08. Branch `feat/v2-bespoke`, off `preview/design-v2`. The founder approved
productionising the 2026-10-07 spike (Codex-written GSAP + SVG scenes keyed to the
narration). README's "v2 bespoke scenes" section describes what was built; this records
what it did on real decks and what is still short. Preview:
`~/.blackhole/DeckSmith/2026-10-07/preview/bespoke/index.html` (served at
`http://localhost:8799/bespoke/`).

## What was built

- `build --design v2 --bespoke` (off by default; classic and plain v2 emit their old
  bytes, except the equation-walk fix below, which moves v2 only).
- Deterministic selection of 4-6 beats (`src/bespoke/select.ts`) plus an optional planner
  hint (`plan --bespoke`).
- Per beat: cache → draft → static contract → probe deck and gates → one critique with the
  contact sheet attached → static contract → probe and gates → keep, keep the draft, or
  fall back. Caps on calls (12) and wall time (1800s), checked before every call; a quota
  answer stops every call.
- Safety in three layers: an acorn allowlist walk, a CSP meta in compositions that carry a
  generated scene, and a sandboxed `<decksmith-player>` frame. Paper text is fenced as
  untrusted in the prompts.
- Three motion gates (`src/verify/scenes.ts`): `static_hold`, `graphic_crosses_text`,
  `seek_order`.
- Content-addressed cache with gate verdicts.
- The equation-walk `immediateRender` bug `seek_order` found, fixed under v2.

What happened along the way, all of it in the build logs beside each deck:

- **ko run 1** fell back on b07: `seek_order` 644px between a cold page and a warm one.
  The two frames are identical by eye; the difference is antialiasing on the headline,
  which the shell's own entrance moved. The tolerance went from 200 to 1500px (the real
  leaks measured 3,299-22,037px) and cached rejections now carry the gates' version, so
  run 2 regenerated b07 and nothing else (2 calls).
- **en run 1** exited 1 before any call: the pass staged the deck through `planCut` without
  `onBeatError`, and a stack beat that cannot fit threw. Fixed (and any error in the pass
  now leaves every beat on its archetype); a test pins it.
- **en s10**: the critique's scene leaked state (`seek_order`, 3,299px on a cold page), so
  the pass kept the draft, which had passed.
- **zh s8, s11**: both critique calls ran into the wall-time cap (each call's timeout is
  what is left of the pass) and were killed; both drafts had passed, so they were kept.
  The pass ran 52s past its cap: the cap stops new calls, and the final probe ran after.
- **Rebuild from cache** (en): 0 calls, `index.html` and `deck.html` byte-identical to the
  first build. That rebuild's `verify` reported one `console_error` from hyperframes'
  check ("The AudioContext encountered an error from the audio device"), on bytes that had
  passed an hour earlier — an environment error, not the deck.

## Looking at the frames

Contact sheets for every chosen beat are in the preview, v2 above bespoke, at every cue end.

- Every generated scene builds with its narration and keeps changing through every cue;
  none holds still the way v2's split-compare beats do for 4-5s cues.
- They are correct and legible, and still look like technical drafting rather than
  motion design: 40-56px labels, thin strokes, boxes and arrows. en's perception-action
  loop and zh's training-stage timeline are the strongest; ko's b07 (cosine angle) is the
  most "explanatory".
- ko was drawn with prompt v1, and its scenes leave the right third of the frame empty;
  b05 also shows stray arrowheads of paths not yet drawn (a marker renders on a 0% path).
  Prompt v2 asked for 80% of the box and for hidden paths until they draw; en and zh, drawn
  with v2, fill more of the width, and no stray markers were seen. No gate checks either.

## What still falls short

- **Time.** 13-31 minutes and 540-705k tokens a deck. It cannot run inside HypePaper's
  300s build step. It needs its own budget, off the request path, and it shares the quota
  that stopped decks on 2026-09-08..12.
- **The look.** Correct but plain. The next lever is art direction in the prompt (a few
  reference scenes), not more gates.
- **Gates that do not exist.** Nothing measures how much of the box a scene uses, nor
  stray markers, nor whether the picture is the right picture for the sentence; the
  critique round and a person do. The static walk can be evaded by assembling a property
  name at run time; the CSP and the sandbox are what stop that from reaching the network or
  the host, and the sandbox isolates only a deck served from another origin.
- **Not verified:** a full mp4 render of a bespoke deck (`render`), `drift` across two
  renders, ja decks, packs other than blueprint/signal/atlas, run-to-run variance (each beat
  generated once), and the deck player's hold windows on a real device (only headless).
- Paced decks (`--duration`/`--speed` other than 1×) and non-16:9 formats are skipped.
- 529px on one KaTeX glyph of v2's narrated equation-walk under one seek history, below the
  tolerance and not traced.
