Embedding a DeckSmith deck

Everything on this page is what a third party writes. One module, one element, and one thing you have to know — where the deck is.

The numbered buttons are stops, not slides. A deck pauses several times inside one slide, so the counter shows the deck’s own slide number and the stop index side by side.
